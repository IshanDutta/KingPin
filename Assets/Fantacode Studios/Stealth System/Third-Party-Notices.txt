Placeholder Dialogue Audio

The AI voice lines in this package are placeholders. They were generated with Chatterbox, an open source text-to-speech model released by Resemble AI under the MIT License.

Included Audio Files:
- All dialogue clips under Demo/_Shared/Dialogues, covering the trespassing warnings, the suspicious and alerted investigation barks, and the combat barks.

These clips exist so the demo scenes have something to play while you evaluate the system.
